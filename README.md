# TrustCore AI Systems Oy

**Control before consequence.**

TrustCore is a Finnish company developing governance and action-boundary infrastructure for agentic AI.

As AI systems gain the ability to use tools and affect the world, a central question becomes unavoidable: **when are they allowed to act?**

Our work focuses on explicit authorization, policy enforcement, human control and auditability at the boundary between a proposed action and its consequences.

## Projects in development

Our project portfolio includes **FlowCore**, **FlowRouter**, **GhostRouter** and **Autofix**.

These projects are under development. Detailed project information, technical specifications and implementation details are not being published at this stage. Further announcements will follow as the projects are ready to be introduced publicly.

This repository is the public introduction to TrustCore and a place for future project updates.

## Governance Before Decision

We are also working on *Governance Before Decision*, a book exploring authorization, accountability and human control in AI systems.
